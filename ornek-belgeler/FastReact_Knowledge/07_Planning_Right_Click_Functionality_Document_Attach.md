## Document attach

[Screenshot: RC HL Documents]

This option highlights whether or not the selected Order or Product has documents attached to it. The Right Click Functionality has two Document Attach options, one for Orders and one for Products. Clicking on either of these will display the Document Attach dialog.

A grayed out icon means that there are no documents attached and a coloured icon means that there are documents attached.
