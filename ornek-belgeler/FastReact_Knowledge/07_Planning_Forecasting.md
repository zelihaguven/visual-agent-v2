## Forecasting

Forecasts are built up from the lowest level (Leaf) and will “roll up” to the higher levels (Branches). These lowest level Leaves will be associated with a FastReact Product which will allow for the definition of routing and bill of material (BoM). A Product may not represent a finished (sold) SKU but may be a generic Type, Family or Sub-Family representing several different finished (sold) SKU’s. In this way a forecast may be entered for a Family with an average route and average BoM.

### Forecasting

**Save (button):** - [Icon: Save report] Save Forecast.

This button will save the altered Forecast.

**Expand All (button):** - [Icon: Expand] Expand all branches.

This button will expand all of the Forecast branches.

**Contract All (button):** - [Icon: Contract] Contract all branches.

This button will contract all of the Forecast branches.

**Expand selected branch (button):** - [Icon: expand branch] Expand selected branch.

This button will expand the selected Forecast branch.

**Contract selected branch (button):** - [Icon: contract branch] Contract selected branch.

This button will contract the selected Forecast branch.

**Toggle unit measurement (button):** - [Icon: work contect or units] Toggle unit measurement.

This drop down menu allows the user to switch the Forecast between using the Work Content and the Unit quantity in the loading calculations.

**Show consolidated charts for this period (button):** - [Icon: Select period] Selected period.

This drop down button allows the user to select a Period in which to view the consolidated charts.

**Probability from (button):** This is the starting percentage probability of the Forecast Orders being turned in to a Confirmed Order.

**to (button):** This is the finishing percentage probability of the Forecast Orders being turned in to a Confirmed Order.

**Locked only (button):** Show only the locked Forecast orders in the probability range.

#### Forecast Plan

This is a visual plan of the Forecast Orders in Evolve/Align showing:

| Forecast orders | The imported or manually enter Forecast quantity for the Product. |
|---|---|
| **Linked orders** | The quantity of imported orders that are linked to the Forecast. |
| **Unlinked orders** | The quantity of orders that have not been linked and are outside of the Forecast. |
| **Planned orders** | The quantity of Linked and Unlinked orders that are planned. |
| **Tent Reserve** | The quantity that is tentatively in Reserve. |
| **Conf Reserve** | The quantity that is confirmed in Reserve. |
| **Unconsumed f/c** | The quantity of the Forecast remaining that is not consumed by any of the above. |
