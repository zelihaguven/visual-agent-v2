## Embellishment Planning

Embellishment Planning is the numerical interface of the graphical planning boards normally associated with FastReact. Although a graphical representation of the strip can be seen on the Embellishment Planning dialog, this planning dialogs main advantage lies in the fact that the planners can type in a numeric schedule. The dialog can be accessed via the Right Click option on the High Level Planning board or by pressing F5 on the selected strip.

This allows the planner to see and alter the requirements for each day. This becomes more evident when planning in multiple sections, for instance Operation1 in Section1 feeds Operation2 in Section2.
