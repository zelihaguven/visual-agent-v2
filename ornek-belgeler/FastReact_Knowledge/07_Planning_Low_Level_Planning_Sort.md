## Sort strips

### Low Level Planning Board

- [Icon: LL sort strips] Sort strips.

This buttons allows the planner to sort an individual planning row or the whole plane by a number of option such as: -

- Delivery date
- Product
- Product types
- Order status
- Translated order status
