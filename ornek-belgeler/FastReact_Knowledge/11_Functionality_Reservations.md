## Reservations

The Material Reservation functionality allows the User to reserve either a Material Delivery or Material Stock or a combination of both to a particular Order or group of Orders. The reservation quantity will then not be included in MRP calculations unless the calculation is done against the Order or Orders to which that Material is reserved.

Reserving a Material for a Order or group of Orders means that the Material quantity reserved cannot be used on any other Order.
