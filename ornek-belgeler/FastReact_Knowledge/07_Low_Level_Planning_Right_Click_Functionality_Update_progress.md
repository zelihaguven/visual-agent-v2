## Update progress

[Screenshot: LL RC update progress]

When selected the dialog screen below will open.

Within this screen the planner can update the non planned and the planned operations. The Planned operation is highlighted in yellow on this dialog when the Planning board is open. This lets the planner know that the update will not be applied to the operation until the Planning Board is closed.
