## S trip Colour Codes

Strip colours are setup on the Plan Colours tab of the Planning option. As new functionality is added to FastReact some strip colours may appear as black. In the first instance, checking the Plan Colours may resolve this issue.

### Flow colours

In addition to the standard strip colours above FastReact can also indicate flow problems (assuming you have flow planning enabled). Flow status is indicated with vertical solid bars. However, the other problems highlighted above (late for delivery, critical path problems) will also be shown as blocks of colour between these bars.

[Screenshot: Strip colours All]

### Materials display

In addition to the standard strip colours and Flow colours above FastReact can also indicate material (MRP) problems (assuming you have MRP planning enabled). Material status is indicated with vertical solid or cross hashed bars. Critical materials (hard constraint) problems are shown as solid colours. Where the problem is confined to non Critical materials (soft constraint) the strip is shown with a cross hatched pattern in the appropriate colour.
