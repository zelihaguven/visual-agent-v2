## Planned schedule

[Screenshot: LL RC planned schedule]

When selected the dialog screen below will open.

 [Screenshot: LL RC planned schedule]

This screen gives the planner the daily rate of production based on the current production plan. The planner can view the production output on a daily, weekly or monthly basis by selecting the relevant option.
