# Main Menu - Planning - Right Click Functionality - Unload

**Source:** FastReact Help (version 4.44) — User manual
**Menu path:** Main Menu > Planning > Right Click Functionality > Unload

---

## Unload strip

[Screenshot: Umload strips]

This right click option will unload the selected strip. Either, in the case of the High Level board, to the High Level load list or, in the case of the Low Level board, to the Low Level load list.
