## High Level Traditional Planning Board

The High Level Planning Board is an infante capacity view of the planned strips.

### Traditional High Level Planning Board

There are two parts to the High Level Planning Board: the strip view and the 'Bucket' view.

#### The Strip view see also Moving Orders

This half of the High Level Planning Board is made visible via the planning Board menu button:

- [Icon: LL planned] View schedule plan

This will provide a view similar to the below image:

[Screenshot: HL Traditional board]

In this view the Planned can also enable the option to 'Do not hide made strips'. This option allows the Planner to see where production updates have been applied against a Strip/Order. These blocks of Green and Red symbolise NET positive and negative updates on that day.

- [Icon: Positive update] NET positive update per day.
- [Icon: Negative update] NET negative update per day.

#### The Mixed view

This half of the High Level Planning Board is made visible via the planning Board menu button:

- [Icon: HL mix view button] View schedule plan and the 'Buckets'

This will provide a view similar to the below image:

[Screenshot: HL mix view]

This shows both the strips and the Capacity/Loading 'Buckets' against each Group. This allows the Planner to see how loaded each group or how much free capacity the group 'Bucket' has.

#### The 'Bucket' view see also Loading Charts

This half of the High Level Planning Board is made visible via the planning Board menu button:

- [Icon: HL Bucket] View 'Buckets'

This will provide a view similar to the below image:

[Screenshot: HL Bucket view]

The 'Bucket' view can also be switched to a Numeric view via the right click and selecting 'Numeric display'.

[Screenshot: HL Bucket Numeric view]
