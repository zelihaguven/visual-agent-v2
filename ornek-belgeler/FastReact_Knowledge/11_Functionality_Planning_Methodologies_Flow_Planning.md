## Flow Planning

FastReact allows an order to be split into multiple deliveries and each of these can be planned as separate items. FastReact will monitor and display the relationship of each planned delivery with its specific delivery date and warns of potential late delivery to the customer, in time, hopefully, to re-plan and correct the problem.

In some circumstances, customers demand a 'flow schedule', normally only for orders which would take many weeks to fulfil. This flow schedule would specify the quantity of the order that is to be made available by the supplier on a week-to-week basis. In this way the customer can be assured that a regular supply of items are made available at its retail outlets and hence maximise the sales opportunity. Whilst good for the customer this regime does place more burden on the supplier to keep to the agreed weekly schedule. To help in this task FastReact has a flow module specifically to facilitate entry and maintenance of flows and to identify problems both visually, on the planning board, and through reporting options.

The driving function for the flow schedule is the Flow Entry Dialog. As this is where flows are defined and saved. This dialog can be accessed by the Strip Menu on the planning board or though the Order Menu on the Main Menu.

The planning board warns of flow problems by means of a colour-coded pattern on the strips. Where no flow problem exists the strips are shown in their normal colours (critical path problems, late against delivery dates etc). However, if any cumulative week's availability falls behind the cumulative agreed flow for the whole order then all strips for that order will be drawn with vertical bars.

See the section on Strip Colours for more details.

### Suggested method of operation

When orders are taken the customer will give an indication of required delivery dates and quantities. Often the whole order will be subdivided into stages so that a small sample can be distributed to retail to gauge public enthusiasm for the product. This would be followed by a bulk delivery for the major part of the order. In turn this may also be followed by a 'top up' quantity to be confirmed when the level of sales of the product is known.

Initially each of these parts (deliveries) can be planned on FastReact in a way that maximises the on time delivery of each part of the order. At this stage the Flow Entry Dialog can be opened and the current plan copied into the Agreed flow. This is then offered to the customer and if approved this Agreed flow is saved as the Original flow. From this point the supply performance can be gauged by the visual warnings on the planning board and though the reporting options relating to order flows.
