## Main Menu

The FastReact main menu has the options listed in the following topics:

- Exit
- Products
- Orders
- Customers
- Update
- Reports
- Materials
- Planning
- Setup
- Tools
- Repeat
- Help

Not all of these will be active or even visible at any one time as this will very much depend on a number of factors such as:

- The options enabled within the terms of your Company's license;
- The security options applicable to the User Group to which you belong;
- The current states of your FastReact session, as certain options are mutually exclusive with others.
