## Retrieve distributed plan

Sometimes known as Plug Out, Distributed Planning is a way of planning on a multi site environment.

### Retrieve

This option allows the contents of returned distributed plans to be incorporated into your host plan.

**Retrieve replies (button):** Click here and FastReact will check the email account that you have defined. These will be listed below.

**Replies waiting:** This is a list of all Distributed Plan (Plug Out) replies that are waiting to be processed.

**Process replies (button):** All replies that are waiting will be processed and incorporated into the host plan.

**Recovery (button):** This option need never be used. It is provided primarily for testing purposes.

> **Note:** A zero Work Content change, at the Plug Out site, will not be reincorporated back into the Host system.
