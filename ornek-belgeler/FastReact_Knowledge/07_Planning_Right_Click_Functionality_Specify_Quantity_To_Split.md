## Specify quantity to split

[Screenshot: Qty to Split]

This option allows the planner to select the amount that they wish to split the strip by. Selecting this option results in the dialog bellow. This dialog has the maximum amount on the strip and the automatically selected split amount. This amount will change depending upon where the cursor was when the Specify quantity to split option was chosen.
