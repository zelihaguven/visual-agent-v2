## Strips on the board

At the beginning of each row there may be a white, red or blue box. This box gives a very basic overview of some information help against that row.

[Screenshot: LL Plan]

- A number in a white box represents the amount of strips planned on that row.
- A number in a red box represents the accumulation of days late for delivery.
- A number in a blue box represents the accumulation of days late for critical path.

Clicking on any of these boxes the dialog box below will appear.

[Screenshot: LL Plan]

| Option 1 | Accumulation of day late for delivery. |
|---|---|
| Option 2 | Accumulation of days late on critical path. |
