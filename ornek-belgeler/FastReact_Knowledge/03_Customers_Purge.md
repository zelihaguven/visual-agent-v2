## Purge Customers

The purge facility allows the removal of all customers for which there is no current order or product. This option should be used periodically to clean up your database, especially where orders are taken from customers who are unlikely to generate repeat business.

### Purge

**Purge (button):** This button will purge all the Customers in FastReact. After the button is clicked a dialog will appear which will display a count of the customers that would be removed and give the option of cancelling the purge operation.
