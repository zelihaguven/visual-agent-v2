# Main Menu - Planning - High Level Planning - Moving Orders

**Source:** FastReact Help (version 4.44) — User manual
**Menu path:** Main Menu > Planning > High Level Planning > Moving Orders

---

## Moving Orders

Strips on the High Level planning board can be moved both in time and to different planning groups. This is achieved by clicking with the mouse and dragging the strip to the required position on the board. FastReact will use the rules that have been defined to prevent loading on groups that are unsuitable for the order and/or product, and the strip will display its status as the changes are made. The capacity display at the bottom of the screen will show the loading on the group on which the selected order is planned.

### Costing Information

This information entered on the Financial tab within Products and/or Orders Add/Amend dialogs will be displayed, when the strip is picked up from the board or the load list, in the group list to allow the planner to make an informed planning decision.

Within the set up of the High Level Planning Board there is an option to ‘Filter groups by valid costings’. If this option is enabled, FastReact will only show groups on which the order can be planned.

### Moving orders in time

Click on the strip and drag it to the left or right. Alternatively the planner can press the left and right arrow keys whilst holding down the Shift key. This will move the selected strip a week at a time in the appropriate direction.

When the strip is put down again, it must be put back onto the row from which it was picked up. To help here FastReact will underline the picked row so the planner can easily find it again.

If the picked strip will not be moving to another planning group (see below) then press the Shift key when the strip is picked up. There is no need to hold the Shift key down once the strip has been picked up. Now FastReact will keep the mouse icon on the selected row and only allow left and right movement. When the strip is dropped again the mouse movement restriction will be cleared.

### Moving orders to another group

Click on the required strip and drag over the loading charts. FastReact will hide any groups that are not suitable for the selected order/product making re-planning easier. The position and status of the selected strip is shown by a double headed arrow on the top of the chart. The colour of this marker will show whether the strip is planned early (against its critical path) or late (against its delivery date).

### Changing production time

When an order is first loaded onto the planning board its length will be the default number of days that have been defined. This can be changed whilst planning by picking up the strip and pressing the left or right arrow keys whilst holding down the Ctrl key. The strip will get longer or shorter as appropriate. The loading charts will reflect the new daily output schedule.

### Multiple selection

Many strips can also be moved at once by using the multiple selection option. To do this just right click on one of the strips that require to be move. The top option on the pop up menu is 'Multi select strips' and this offers a choice of possible links.

For example, all strips for a product or customer.

Select the required option and FastReact will display just those selected strips on the top part of the planning board and restrict the list of groups below to just those that can handle your selection.

### Moving in time

The same rules apply to moving multiple strips as described above. The starting position of selected strips relative to each other will be maintained when they are moved.

### Moving orders to another group

Multiple strips can be dragged to another group and they will all be moved together, irrespective of their starting group. Caution is advised though, as orders may have been selected that cannot be planned on the new group. These orders will be returned to the load list for re-planning later.

The starting position of all orders that are moved across the loading charts will be synchronised.
