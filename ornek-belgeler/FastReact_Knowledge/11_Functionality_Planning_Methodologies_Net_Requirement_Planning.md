## Net Requirement Planning

Occasionally called Plan Line, Net Requirement Planning or MPS allows FastReact to turn a sales forecast into a requirement to make.

As well as planning purchase orders at a supplier or works orders at a factory, FastReact can calculate what purchase orders (PO’s) and / or works orders (WO’s) are required in the first place. This module is normally applicable to branded or make to stock businesses.

Firstly FastReact calculates the net demand (usually on a week by week basis) based on a combination of forecast and sales orders and a company specific demand policy. The demand policy is usually highly specific to each FastReact client and is programmed specifically to meet the needs of that business.

For example 'make the greater of forecast or customer orders in any week but roll any difference into the following week'.

Secondly FastReact calculates the net requirements by location based on existing supply (stock and existing PO’s / WO’s) plus the ordering policy (safety stock, minimum and incremental order quantities).

FastReact then automatically creates suggested planning orders (Suggested PO’s or suggested WO’s) that meet the net requirements. These can then be manually planned on the FastReact planning board or, more typically, automatically planned using the FastReact APS module. Users can then decide which suggested orders to convert into confirmed PO’s or WO’s.
